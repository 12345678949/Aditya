Ayurvedic Eye Care

Explore holistic and natural Ayurvedic remedies for eye health, based on centuries of traditional knowledge.

Disclaimer: Always consult with a qualified Ayurvedic practitioner and an ophthalmologist before trying any new treatments.

Key Ayurvedic Practices

Triphala Eyewash

An eyewash made from Triphala (a combination of three fruits: Amalaki, Bibhitaki, and Haritaki) is traditionally used to reduce eye strain, combat infections, and soothe redness. Its antioxidant properties help protect the eyes.

Netra Tarpana

This is a rejuvenating treatment where a dough ring is placed around the eyes and filled with lukewarm medicated ghee (Ghrita). It is believed to nourish the eye tissues, improve vision, and relieve dryness and fatigue.

Diet and Lifestyle

Ayurveda emphasizes a diet rich in Vitamin A and antioxidants. Including foods like carrots, leafy greens, almonds, and amla (Indian gooseberry) can support long-term eye health. Reducing screen time and practicing eye exercises are also recommended.

Active Compounds in Remedies

Key molecules like Gallic Acid (from Triphala) and Phenethyl Alcohol (from rose water) have known antioxidant and antimicrobial properties.
